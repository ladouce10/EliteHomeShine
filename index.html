Our Services

Professional cleaning for every kind of home

Choose the level of care your space needs. We offer flexible residential cleaning services for routine upkeep, detailed resets and special transitions.

Standard Cleaning

Reliable routine cleaning for homes that are already in generally good condition. Ideal for weekly, bi-weekly or monthly upkeep.

  • Kitchen & bathroom surfaces
  • Dusting & surface wipe-down
  • Vacuuming & mopping
  • Trash emptied

Deep Cleaning

A detailed top-to-bottom reset for first-time visits, seasonal cleaning or homes with built-up dust and grime.

  • Everything in Standard Cleaning
  • Detailed baseboards & edges
  • Doors, frames & switches
  • Extra kitchen & bathroom detail

Move-In / Move-Out

Thorough cleaning for empty or mostly empty homes so the space is ready for its next chapter.

  • Inside cabinets & drawers
  • Interior appliance cleaning
  • Baseboards, doors & trim
  • Detailed kitchens & bathrooms

Post-Renovation Cleaning

Detailed cleaning after renovation or construction work to remove fine dust and leave surfaces move-in ready.

  • Fine dust removal
  • Accessible surface wipe-down
  • Vacuuming & floor cleaning
  • Detailed finishing touches

Airbnb / Turnover Cleaning

Efficient between-stay cleaning designed to help your property feel fresh, tidy and guest-ready.

  • Kitchen & bathroom reset
  • Dusting and floors
  • Trash removal
  • Guest-ready finishing touches

Cleaning Add-Ons

Customize your appointment with extra-detail tasks when your home needs more than the base service.

  • Inside oven or refrigerator
  • Inside cabinets
  • Interior windows & sills
  • Blinds & detailed baseboards

FAQ

Questions before you book?

Here are a few helpful answers about our cleaning services.

What is the difference between Standard and Deep Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning is designed for regular upkeep of a home that is already in generally good condition. Deep Cleaning includes the standard service plus additional detail work such as baseboards, edges, doors, frames, switches and more attention to built-up grime.
Do you offer move-in and move-out cleaning?
Yes. This service is designed for empty or mostly empty homes and includes detailed kitchens and bathrooms, inside cabinets and drawers, interior appliance cleaning, baseboards, trim and accessible surfaces.
How long does a cleaning take?
Timing depends on the size and condition of the home and the service selected. Deep cleanings and special-detail services generally take longer than routine maintenance cleaning.
